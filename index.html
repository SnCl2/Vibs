Practical Vastu Analysis for Your Property

Every property is different. Therefore, a systematic, step-by-step approach is followed to understand the property before suitable corrections are recommended.

My Vastu Methodology

1

SURROUNDING ENVIRONMENT

The surroundings of the property are first examined, and the conditions on all four sides are studied.
Roads, neighbouring structures, open spaces, and other external factors may be considered during the assessment.

2

PROPERTY PLAN

The property layout is carefully prepared and studied to understand the exact arrangement of the building.

3

16–32 ZONE ANALYSIS

The property is divided into 16–32 Vastu Zones. Each zone is examined individually to understand its condition and the activities or elements located within it.

4

IMPORTANT AREAS

The Main Entrance and other entrances are analysed along with important areas such as: Entrance, Kitchen, Bedrooms ,Bathrooms, Living Room ,DiningArea, Puja Area Staircase .Study Area .Other Functional Areas .

5

COLOURS & OBJECTS

The colours used in different areas and the placement of important objects and equipment are also examined, including: Refrigerator ,Washing Machine, Electrical Equipment ,Furniture, Other Significant Objects .

6

IDENTIFY & CORRECT

After the assessment has been completed, areas requiring attention are identified and suitable Vastu correction methods are developed according to the specific conditions of the property.

7

ASTRO & NUMERO-VASTU

Where appropriate, Astro-Vastu and Numero-Vastu may be incorporated as additional considerations within the overall Vastu assessment.

8

95-DAY OBSERVATION

After the recommended corrections have been implemented, a 95-Day Observation may be conducted to review relevant developments and observations.

9

DEVTA BALANCING

Where required, Devta Balancing may be recommended as part of the overall Vastu process.
