ABOUT JAYANTA ACHARYA

Continuing a Family Tradition of Astrology
Jayanta Acharya is a Vedic Astrologer and Astrology Consultant, providing astrological consultancy since 2006.

Astrology is not simply regarded as a profession but as a family tradition that has been passed down through generations. The practice of astrology was first carried forward by his grandfather, followed by his father, and is now being continued by him.

The consultations are based on the traditional principles of Vedic Astrology and Vishuddha Siddhanta. Over 20 years of practice, astrological consultancy has been provided to clients from different parts of India as well as to clients living abroad.

Through years of consultation, numerous individual birth charts have been studied, and guidance has been provided based on planetary positions, Dashas, Yogas, and other traditional astrological considerations.
